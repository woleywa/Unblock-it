Happy Blocks
Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · applies to the Happy Blocks website, iPhone/iPad app and Android app.

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We don’t sell or share your data with anyone for advertising.
  • You can play without an account. Your progress then stays on your device.
  • If you pick a nickname or join a team or challenge, your nickname and scores are public to other players.
  • If you add an email and password, your email stays private and is only used to sign you in.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

Who we are

Happy Blocks is a puzzle game made by an independent developer (GitHub: woleywa). In this policy “we” means the maker of Happy Blocks. For questions, see Contact.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores the online data above, and Firebase Hosting serves the website version (Google may log IP addresses for security and abuse prevention). Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game’s font. Your device downloads it from Google, which sees your IP address as part of that request (Google Fonts privacy).
  • GitHub hosts the game’s code, forwards the old website address (github.io) to the new one, and serves the game updates the apps download; no personal data is sent with those requests. GitHub may log IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).
  • If you install the app from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google handle the download under their own privacy policies.

Data stored with these services may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

Why we’re allowed to use it

We use your data only to run the game and the features you choose to use (legal basis: performing our service for you, and our legitimate interest in keeping leaderboards fair). Creating a nickname, team, challenge or account is always optional.

How long we keep it

  • Everything linked to your account stays until you delete the account.
  • Results in challenges you joined stay with that challenge after it ends, so its scoreboard remains complete.
  • Data on your device stays until you delete the app, clear the website’s data, or sign out.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the game, tap your name → Delete my account (as a guest: Delete my nickname and scores). This removes your login, email, nickname, totals, level records, saved progress, friends list and team membership. Progress kept only on your device is removed by deleting the app or clearing the website’s data.
  • Change your nickname or team name at any time, and leave a team from the team page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its use. Contact us and we’ll help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Happy Blocks is a game for everyone, but it isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. Kids can play as a guest without giving any personal information. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each player change only their own data, and private data (email, progress copy, friends list) can only be read by its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/woleywa/Unblock-it/issues (please don’t post your email address or password there — we’ll reply with a private way to reach us).
